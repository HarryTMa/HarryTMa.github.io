About关于

I'm a Master of Finance student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University's Antai College of Economics and Management. My research interests center on the intersection of artificial intelligence and finance, as well as large language models. I received my undergraduate dual degree in Finance and Computer Science from SJTU. 上海交通大学安泰经济与管理学院金融硕士。研究方向聚焦于人工智能与金融的交叉，以及大语言模型。本科毕业于上海交通大学金融–计算机双学位。
